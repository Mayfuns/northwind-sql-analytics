# Northwind Analytics — PostgreSQL Business Analysis

A PostgreSQL project analysing the Northwind relational business dataset across **customers, orders, products, suppliers, employees, categories and sales performance**.

[View the supporting project](https://mayfuns.github.io/mariam-analytics-portfolio/supporting-projects.html#northwind)

## SQL analysis file

[View the complete Northwind analysis](01_sql/Northwind%20SQL%20Project.sql)

## Analysis areas

### Customers & orders
- Orders placed by customers in specific countries
- Customers with and without orders
- Order counts by customer
- Customers with at least five orders
- Top customers by order count
- Top customers by purchase value
- Customers ordering in 1998

### Products, categories & suppliers
- Products supplied by suppliers in specific countries
- Most expensive products
- Product counts by category
- Categories with more than 10 products
- Average product price by category
- Suppliers and number of products supplied
- Suppliers with more than 500 units sold
- Total inventory value by category
- Top products by sales revenue

### Employees & sales
- Orders processed by employees
- Orders handled by each employee
- Employees processing more than 100 orders
- Total sales generated by employee
- Employees with more than $50,000 in sales

### Relational completeness
- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- RIGHT JOIN
- FULL OUTER JOIN
- Identification of unmatched records

### Combined business lists
- Customer and supplier cities
- Customer and supplier contacts
- Countries represented across the database
- `UNION` and `UNION ALL`
- Frequency counts across combined datasets

## SQL techniques demonstrated

- PostgreSQL
- INNER, LEFT, RIGHT and FULL OUTER JOIN
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- COUNT, SUM and AVG
- DISTINCT
- Filtering and sorting
- Subqueries
- COALESCE
- EXTRACT
- UNION and UNION ALL
- Revenue calculations with quantity and discount
- Inventory-value calculations
- Top-N analysis

## Database setup

The repository also includes the Northwind relational schema and seeded data in the database setup file, giving the analytical queries a complete reproducible foundation.
